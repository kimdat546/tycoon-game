Here’s a complete concept, story, and feature set for your “Infinity Gacha”-inspired hero management, dungeon, and town-building game—a blend of Infinite Gacha’s narrative depth and Abyss Hunter Tycoon’s simulation elements.

Game Title: "Eternal Tower: Infinity Gacha Chronicles"
Genre
Idle RPG / Gacha Hero Collection / Town & Dungeon Management

Story Overview
In "Eternal Tower: Infinity Gacha Chronicles," players are “Masters” summoned into an infinitely tall dungeon tower tied to mysterious ancient magic. Each Master awakens inside the game world as a lowly 1-star hero with memories of their former life as a top player. To return home, Masters must ascend the 100 floors of the Eternal Tower by recruiting, nurturing, and commanding a growing cast of heroes—each with unique personalities, backgrounds, and secrets.

But there’s a twist: every hero in this world is self-aware, bearing their own dreams and histories. Heroes are not mere pawns—they interact, form bonds, and react to players’ leadership, as inspired by "Pick Me Up: Infinite Gacha." Choices made in training, deployment, and team composition influence both their combat strength and story arcs.

Core Gameplay Features
Hero Summoning and Management
Gacha System: Summon heroes with varying star rarity (1-5 stars), classes (Warrior, Mage, Healer, Rogue, etc.), and elemental affinities. Rarer heroes have higher growth but all can be made strong through care.

Hero Growth: Level up, train, equip, and “ascend” heroes through combat and daily routines (farming, smithing, studying magic, etc.).

Bond System: Heroes build relationships with the Master and each other, unlocking special skills, backstory quests, and team bonuses.

Town/Tower Building
Base Management: Upgrade tower floors into specialized facilities (training grounds, alchemy labs, blacksmiths, resource farms).

Automation: Assign heroes to work in facilities, producing resources even while offline. Idle rewards are collected at the next login.

Resource System: Balance currencies (gold, mana, rare materials) for hero upgrades, building expansions, and advanced summons.

Dungeon Exploration
Floor Progression: Tackle increasingly challenging dungeon floors with waves of enemies, elite minibosses, and unique traps.

Tactical Combat: Form hero squads, strategize with elemental/skill synergies, and use manual skills or auto-battling.

Boss Encounters: Face powerful floor guardians; some bosses may attempt to recruit, trick, or form rivalries with the player's heroes.

Narrative and Choice
Branching Quests: Player choices shape hero development and the world’s fate. Some heroes may leave, transform, or even betray, depending on your actions.

Personal Stories: Each hero has side quests and evolving relationships; unlocking their memories gives lore, permanent stat boosts, and special endings.

Meta Narrative: The Master's knowledge of “game mechanics” can sometimes bend the rules—unlocking unique cheats, exploits, or ethical dilemmas.

Progression & Replayability
Ascension System: After reaching the 100th floor, start “Ascension”—resetting progress for persistent meta-bonuses, new hero types, and harder dungeons.

Seasonal Events: Rotating special dungeons, global leaderboards, unique cosmetic and resource rewards.

Prestige & Collection: Collect every hero, max out bonds, and discover rare “glitched” summons.

Monetization (Optional)
Cosmetics/Premium Currency: Optional for unique hero skins, fast-tracking resource generation, or special event gachas—no pay-to-win.

Example Opening (Story Intro)
Awakening with a splitting headache, the player finds themselves inside a crumbling chamber on the first floor of the Eternal Tower. A mysterious voice echoes—a “System Admin,” inviting the new Master to assemble a team from the scraps of fallen worlds. The first summon produces a lowly 1-star farmer…but there’s determination in their eyes. As the Master and their heroes rise through the tower, bonds form, secrets are uncovered, rivals are faced, and the path home grows clearer—but at what cost?
